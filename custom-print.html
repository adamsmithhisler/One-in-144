Custom 3D Printing

Upload an original design or a design you have permission to reproduce and request a limited custom print for your next model or diorama project.

How It Works

1. Upload Your Design

Select a supported STL or OBJ model file for your custom printing request.

2. Select Print Options

Choose the material, quantity, intended scale, and provide any necessary project notes.

3. Review Before Ordering

Custom designs require review and pricing before a printing order can be approved.

Print Request

Accepted formats: STL and OBJ. File processing is not active in this prototype.

Print Guidelines

  • Orders are intended for personal hobby projects.
  • Quantity is limited to small production runs.
  • Designs must be original or authorized for reproduction.
  • Oversized or unusually complex designs may not be accepted.
  • Pricing and approval will be reviewed before checkout.

Prototype Notice: File processing, automatic pricing, and actual order submission are not active during this stage of development.
