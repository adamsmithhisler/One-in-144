Print Guidelines

  • Custom printing is intended for small-scale hobby projects.
  • Quantity is limited to 1–10 pieces per request.
  • Only original or authorized designs may be submitted.
  • Complex geometry, dimensions, and print feasibility require review.
  • Custom estimates do not include shipping or checkout discounts.

Classroom Prototype: Estimates are illustrative and are not based on a real model-volume analysis. Selecting a design file does not upload it. Adding a request to the Smart Cart does not create a real order or approve a print job.
